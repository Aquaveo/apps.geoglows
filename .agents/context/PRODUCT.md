# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers, hydrologists, water resource engineers, and academics working on water sustainability challenges. They arrive to access forecasting, groundwater analysis, and water intelligence tools. Their context ranges from field research to policy preparation; they need to find the right tool quickly, trust the data behind it, and get to work without friction.

## Product Purpose

GEOGLOWS apps.geoglows is the portal and app library for the Global Water Sustainability initiative. It connects users to open-access water intelligence tools (river forecasting, groundwater monitoring, aquifer analysis) built on global datasets. Success looks like: a researcher finds the right tool in seconds, trusts it immediately, and starts productive work without onboarding friction.

## Brand Personality

Open, Approachable, Empowering

The voice is direct and warm without being casual. It respects the user's expertise while remaining welcoming to newcomers. No jargon walls, no corporate polish, no academic gatekeeping.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS dashboards** (Stripe, Linear): too corporate and transactional for an open science platform. GEOGLOWS is a public good, not a product being sold.
- **Dense academic data portals** (USGS, legacy NASA tools): powerful but intimidating, buried navigation, walls of controls. GEOGLOWS should feel lighter and more navigable.
- **Flashy marketing sites**: animation-heavy, scroll-hijacking, hero-metric templates. This portal serves tools, not conversions.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the work, not the wrapper**: tools and data are the value. Every design choice should reduce the distance between the user and their work.
2. **Credible without being cold**: scientific credibility comes from clarity and precision, not from sterile aesthetics or institutional heaviness.
3. **Welcome everyone in**: an undergraduate seeing GEOGLOWS for the first time and a senior hydrologist returning for the hundredth time should both feel oriented.
4. **Global by nature**: the platform serves users across geographies, institutions, and connectivity levels. Design for the widest reasonable audience.
5. **Quiet confidence**: let the data and tools speak. The interface stays out of the way.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Ensure sufficient color contrast ratios, full keyboard navigation, screen-reader-friendly markup, and respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`. Consider users on varying connection speeds and screen sizes given the global user base.
